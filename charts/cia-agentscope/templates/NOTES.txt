1. Inspect the application Service:
   kubectl get service {{ include "cia-agentscope.fullname" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

2. Wait for the application rollout:
   kubectl rollout status deployment/{{ include "cia-agentscope.fullname" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} --timeout=10m

3. Forward the ClusterIP Service and check application health:
   kubectl port-forward service/{{ include "cia-agentscope.fullname" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} 7010:{{ .Values.service.port }}
   curl http://127.0.0.1:7010/health

OpenSandbox is not installed by this Chart. Functions that require a sandbox
remain unavailable until OpenSandbox is deployed separately and its connection
settings are updated with helm upgrade.
